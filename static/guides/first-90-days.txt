YOUR FIRST 90 DAYS ABROAD
A practical starter guide from Kawan

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE
- Keep digital and printed copies of your passport, visa, admission letter, insurance, and accommodation details.
- Save your university's international office and local emergency contacts.
- Plan a first-month budget that includes transport, food, phone service, deposits, and basic home supplies.

YOUR FIRST WEEK
- Complete university registration and any local immigration requirements.
- Set up a local phone number, bank account, and public transport access.
- Walk your route to campus before your first class.
- Introduce yourself to one classmate, neighbor, or student group.

YOUR FIRST MONTH
- Attend orientation and visit the international student office.
- Learn how to access health care and wellbeing support.
- Review the work conditions attached to your visa before accepting a job.
- Create a weekly routine that includes study, meals, rest, movement, and connection.

DAYS 31-90
- Join one recurring club, volunteer group, faith community, or sports activity.
- Check your budget and adjust recurring expenses.
- Meet with an academic adviser before small concerns become bigger ones.
- Make time to celebrate what you have already navigated.

IF THINGS FEEL HARD
Culture shock, homesickness, and uncertainty are common parts of transition. Reaching out is a strength. Contact your institution's wellbeing team, a trusted person, or a Kawan mentor.

This guide offers general information, not legal or immigration advice. Always confirm current requirements with your institution and official government sources.
